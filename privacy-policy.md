# Privacy Policy — Medchal Car Care

**Effective Date:** 15 October 2026

Medchal Car Care provides doorstep car washing and vehicle cleaning services in Medchal, Telangana, India.

We respect our customers' privacy and are committed to protecting their personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

When customers contact us or book a service through WhatsApp or our website, we may collect:

- Customer name and WhatsApp phone number
- Service address or vehicle parking location
- Vehicle type and selected cleaning service
- Preferred booking date and time slot
- Booking status, payment status, and relevant customer messages

## 2. How We Use Information

We use customer information to:

- Receive, schedule, and manage car cleaning bookings
- Communicate booking confirmations and service updates
- Coordinate doorstep service delivery
- Maintain booking and payment records
- Respond to customer support requests

We do not sell customers' personal information.

## 3. WhatsApp and Service Providers

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to communicate with customers.

We may use Google Sheets and other necessary technology providers to store and manage bookings. These providers process information in connection with the services they provide.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to restrict unauthorized access to customer information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain customer information only as long as reasonably necessary for booking management, customer support, recordkeeping, and applicable legal obligations.

## 6. Customer Rights and Data Deletion

Customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information by contacting us.

We will review requests and delete eligible information, subject to any applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.

## 7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services change. The latest version will be available on this page.
