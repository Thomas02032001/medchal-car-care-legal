# Data Deletion Instructions — Medchal Car Care

**Last Updated:** 15 October 2026

Medchal Car Care respects your privacy and allows customers to request deletion of their personal information.

## 1. How to Request Data Deletion

If you have contacted Medchal Car Care through WhatsApp or booked a car cleaning service, you may request deletion of your personal information.

Send an email to:

**[INSERT YOUR BUSINESS CONTACT EMAIL]**

**Subject:** Personal Data Deletion Request — Medchal Car Care

Include your name and the WhatsApp phone number used for your booking. Please do not include passwords, payment PINs, or other sensitive credentials.

## 2. Information Eligible for Deletion

Depending on the information we hold and applicable requirements, this may include:

- Customer name and WhatsApp number
- Service address or parking location
- Vehicle and booking details
- Customer communication records under our control

## 3. Request Processing

We will acknowledge deletion requests within 7 business days and aim to complete eligible deletions within 30 days after verifying the request.

If additional time is required, we will inform you.

Certain information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

## 4. Confirmation

Once your request has been processed, we will confirm the outcome through email or another agreed communication channel.
